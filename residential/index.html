Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Before we start any repair, we figure out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check your system's age and tonnage, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get a straightforward recommendation and the complete price up front, whether the job is swapping a weak capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

The trouble with HVAC failures is that most of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil slowly drains refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down a little at a time, and a weak capacitor holds on until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. If your bills are climbing, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, get it looked at while it's still a simple repair.

Not every visit is about a breakdown. Plenty of Westland homeowners ask us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, a replacement on your own timeline beats a rushed swap in the middle of a heat wave. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your equipment has left.

Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for Westland Homes

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service heads off failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At Westland HVAC, every plan is built around your home's actual equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check on the ductwork.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Think of maintenance as protection for equipment you've already paid for. Each tune-up helps keep the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Right-Sized Systems

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

Proper sizing matters more than most homeowners realize. Before recommending new equipment, Westland HVAC measures the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. The result is a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, keeps humidity in check, and holds every room at the temperature you set.

We walk you through the options for most homes, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use dramatically. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Westland

Warm air from the vents, a furnace that won't ignite, a frozen evaporator coil, a slow refrigerant leak: each of these problems has a different cause and needs its own repair. Westland HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that keeps losing its program, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split flashing error codes.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A bad capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, repair leaky ducts that dump conditioned air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
