Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across Ann Arbor

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: older gas furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team works on every one of those systems. We have years of field experience, and we know how the weather here, the age of local homes, and local code affect the way equipment runs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs are usually quick: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Ann Arbor

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
