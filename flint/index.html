When Your System Fails in Flint

A few minutes of caution while you wait for us can prevent a much larger repair. A frozen air conditioner should be shut off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw safely. Running it frozen risks an overflowing drain pan and a damaged compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us right away.

How Flint Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away the moment something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
