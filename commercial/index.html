Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Westland HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable throughout.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Westland

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Westland HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Westland and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Westland HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
